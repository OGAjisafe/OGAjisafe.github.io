Research

Research Interests

My research is in applied microeconomics, with emphasis on industrial organization, sports economics, and agricultural economics. I am particularly interested in competitive responses to institutional changes, market-entry shocks, travel-policy reforms, and agricultural technology adoption.

Working Papers

Charter Flights, Home-Court Advantage, and Betting Market Efficiency in the WNBA

This paper studies the WNBA's league-wide charter-flight policy and examines whether improved travel logistics affected home-court advantage and whether betting markets incorporated these changes into prices.

Swinging at Change: MLB Competitive Response to MLS Expansion

This paper examines how Major League Baseball teams respond strategically to the entry of Major League Soccer franchises into shared metropolitan markets.

The Impact of NWSL Entry on Affiliate MLS Clubs

This study investigates how affiliations between Major League Soccer clubs and National Women's Soccer League teams shape club outcomes and organizational strategy.
